# Sources and rights

TAB-PUB distinguishes the scholarly source used for verification, the electronic text used as a starting point, and the research decisions made by the project.

| Material | Verification source or default apparatus |
| --- | --- |
| M-L, M-A, M-C | The relevant manuscript facsimiles, identified in the research records. |
| G | Rahlfs–Hanhart, Septuaginta, 2006. |
| G-Mss, G-L, G-O, Prelucianic, Lat | By default, Brooke, McLean and Thackeray, The Old Testament in Greek according to the Text of Codex Vaticanus, vol. II, part II: I and II Kings, Cambridge, 1930; individual records can identify another source. |
| V and V-Mss | The relevant Benedictine Vulgate volume and its apparatus. |

| T and T-Mss | The relevant Sperber edition and its apparatus. |

This overview does not replace a reading's precise bibliographic reference and locator. Other material, including DSS attestations where available, requires its own source information.

Original project research and documentation use CC BY 4.0. Discovery metadata is released under CC0 where stated in the deposit. These licenses do not relicense source editions, electronic starting texts or images. Source-specific rights and attributions remain attached to the exported quotations and provenance.

A citation is not itself permission to redistribute an entire source. Publication decisions must consider the actual material being distributed and applicable source terms. The documentation repository does not redistribute full source texts, facsimiles or research backups.
